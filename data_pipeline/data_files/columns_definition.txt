HIST_DATA.csv (15-Year Historical Dataset) — COLUMN DEFINITIONS (EXACT ORDER)
================================================================================

Column Order in output CSV:
---------------------------
 1. SYMBOL                         : Ticker symbol of the Nifty Index (e.g., NSE:NIFTY50-INDEX).
 2. DATETIME                       : Trading date formatted as DD-MMM-YYYY (sorted DESCENDING per symbol).
 3. OPEN                           : Opening price of the trading session.
 4. HIGH                           : Highest price reached during the session.
 5. LOW                            : Lowest price reached during the session.
 6. CLOSE                          : Closing price of the session.
 7. CHANGE                         : Net point change from previous close: CLOSE - PREVIOUS_CLOSE.
 8. CHANGE_PERCENT                 : Percentage return from previous close: ((CLOSE - PREVIOUS_CLOSE) / PREVIOUS_CLOSE) * 100.
 9. TOTAL_LOW_HIGH                 : Absolute High-to-Low daily range in points: HIGH - LOW.
10. GAP                            : Opening gap classification: 'gap up', 'gap down', or 'no gap'.
11. GAP_PERCENT                    : Opening gap percentage: ((OPEN - PREVIOUS_CLOSE) / PREVIOUS_CLOSE) * 100.
12. TOTAL_PREV_LOW_HIGH            : Directional 2-day move span in points:
                                     * Up Day (CLOSE >= PREVIOUS_CLOSE)   : HIGH_today - LOW_prev
                                     * Down Day (CLOSE < PREVIOUS_CLOSE)  : HIGH_prev - LOW_today
13. TOTAL_PREV_LOW_HIGH_PERCENT    : Percentage 2-day move span: ((TOTAL_PREV_LOW_HIGH * 100) / PREVIOUS_CLOSE).
14. UPPER_WICK                     : Size of upper candle wick in points: HIGH - max(OPEN, CLOSE).
15. LOWER_WICK                     : Size of lower candle wick in points: min(OPEN, CLOSE) - LOW.
16. VOLUME                         : Total volume traded during the session (0 if not available).
17. LOW_CLOSE                      : Points distance between Low and Close: CLOSE - LOW.
18. HIGH_CLOSE                     : Points distance between High and Close: CLOSE - HIGH.
19. PREVIOUS_CLOSE                 : Closing price of the previous trading day.
20. HIGH_52W                       : 52-week rolling maximum high (252 trading days).
21. LOW_52W                       : 52-week rolling minimum low (252 trading days).
22. DIST_HIGH52                    : Percentage distance from 52-week High: ((CLOSE - HIGH_52W) / HIGH_52W) * 100.
23. DIST_LOW52                     : Percentage distance from 52-week Low: ((CLOSE - LOW_52W) / LOW_52W) * 100.
24. DAY_NAME                       : Day of the week (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
25. MONTH                          : Month number (1 to 12).
26. QUARTER                        : Quarter number (1 to 4).
27. WEEK                           : ISO calendar week number (1 to 53).
28. RSI_14                         : 14-period Relative Strength Index.
29. VWAP                           : Daily VWAP approximation: (HIGH + LOW + CLOSE) / 3.
30. EMA_20                         : 20-period Exponential Moving Average of CLOSE.
31. EMA_50                         : 50-period Exponential Moving Average of CLOSE.
32. EMA_100                        : 100-period Exponential Moving Average of CLOSE.
33. EMA_200                        : 200-period Exponential Moving Average of CLOSE.
34. EMA_400                        : 400-period Exponential Moving Average of CLOSE.
35. EMA_500                        : 500-period Exponential Moving Average of CLOSE.
36. MACD                           : MACD Line: EMA(12) - EMA(26).
37. MACD_SIGNAL                    : Signal Line: 9-period EMA of MACD.
38. MACD_HIST                      : MACD Histogram: MACD - MACD_SIGNAL.
39. MACD_CROSS                     : MACD Crossover signal ('BULLISH', 'BEARISH', 'NO SIGNAL').
40. MACD_TREND                     : MACD Momentum state ('POSITIVE MOMENTUM', 'NEGATIVE MOMENTUM').
